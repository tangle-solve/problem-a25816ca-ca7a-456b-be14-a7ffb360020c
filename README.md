### ALGO-000017: Frege system lower bounds
- **Subfield:** proof complexity & PCP
- **Open:** Prove superpolynomial lower bounds on proof length in Frege systems (and stronger: bounded-depth Frege, Extended Frege) for any natural tautology family.
- **Status:** Open. No superpolynomial Frege lower bound known for any explicit tautology; resolution is well understood (Haken 1985 for pigeonhole; for all tautologies by Chvátal–Szemerédi).
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Bounds SAT-solver power in the idealized limit; EF lower bounds would imply major circuit lower bounds (P/poly separations).
- **Key works:** A. Haken, "The intractability of resolution", TCS 1985; P. Beame, T. Pitassi, "Propositional proof complexity: past, present, and future", Bull. EATCS 1998
- **Related:** NP vs coNP; circuit lower bounds
